# EngageLens Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

EngageLens: Chat Analytics for Google Meet ("EngageLens") is a browser extension that analyses Google Meet chat for the person using it.

## What EngageLens reads

- **On `meet.google.com` during a call:** the chat messages shown in the call (sender name, message text and time), the meeting title and code, and the display names of the people in the call.
- **Files you choose to import:** Google Meet chat export files (`.sbv`).

EngageLens does not read any other website, your email, your files, or your camera or microphone.

## Where your data goes

Nowhere. All data is processed and stored **locally in your browser** using `chrome.storage.local`. EngageLens:

- makes no network requests and has no servers;
- does not use analytics, advertising or tracking;
- does not sell or transfer your data to anyone;
- does not use your data for anything except showing you meeting reports.

## Keeping and deleting data

Meetings stay in your browser until you delete them. You can delete a single meeting from the EngageLens page. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its data.

## Other participants

Reports contain other people's names and messages from meetings you attended. Use them in line with your organisation's policies, and tell participants when meeting chats are analysed.
